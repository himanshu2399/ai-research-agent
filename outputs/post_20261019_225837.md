### Topic
Checking Kueue admission to diagnose “Pending” Kubernetes Jobs

### Research Summary
- In clusters using Kueue, a Job stuck in Pending often means “not admitted yet” due to quota/policy—not a kube-scheduler or node issue.
- The fastest path to root cause is to read the Workload conditions (QuotaReserved, Admitted) before checking scheduler logs.
- With Headlamp’s Kueue plugin, you can trace Workload → LocalQueue → ClusterQueue → ResourceFlavor to pinpoint capacity or policy blockers.
- Mismatches between a Job’s requests and a ClusterQueue’s quota (e.g., requested CPUs or pod slots exceeding nominal quota) can keep Jobs suspended even when nodes are available.

### LinkedIn Post
If your Kubernetes Job is “Pending” and you’re tailing kube-scheduler logs… you’re probably debugging the wrong system.

In clusters running Kueue, Pending often just means “not admitted yet.” That’s quota/policy, not node scheduling. It’s normal.

The fastest path to root cause isn’t nodes or taints. It’s admission.
- Start at the Workload.
- Read the conditions: QuotaReserved and Admitted.
- Then trace Workload → LocalQueue → ClusterQueue → ResourceFlavor.

Headlamp’s Kueue views make this a quick check.

What you’ll usually find:
- QuotaReserved = false? Your request doesn’t fit the ClusterQueue’s nominal quota (e.g., CPUs or pod slots). The Job stays suspended even if the cluster looks “idle.”
- QuotaReserved = true but Admitted = false? You’ve reserved quota but aren’t admitted yet—policy or flavor selection may be holding the door.
- ResourceFlavor constraints? Policy tied to a specific flavor can block admission.

Why this matters: it reframes “Pending” from “no nodes” to “no capacity according to policy.” That changes your runbook:
- Stop chasing kube-scheduler and node states first.
- Start by checking Workload conditions and queue/quota fit.
- Consider scheduler logs only if the Workload is Admitted and still not running.

As more teams share capacity with Kueue, this admission‑first mental model cuts MTTR and reduces noise.
